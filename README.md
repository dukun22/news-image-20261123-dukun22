# The World Today — 23 November 2026

A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Monday
23 November 2026: the COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya, NATO meets
after Poland confirms three Russian missiles, a Russian warship is sunk
at Benghazi, the Houthis claim a hit on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery,
South Korea's education minister quits over AI exam cheating, and Okinawa's
Shuri Castle reopens seven years after the fire.

The poster is drawn in advance and held in this repository as `entry.png`
(1024×1024, original flat illustration). `main.ts` copies it to
`/output/entry.png`, byte for byte. Every story was verified against
Reuters or AP reporting.

Run it with `node main.ts` — it writes the poster to `/output/entry.png`.
